E-WASTE GUIDE

What Should You Do With Old Electronics?

Choose a responsible next step based on device condition.

Working device

Consider reuse or donation when the device is still functional.

Partially working device

Consider repair or refurbishment before recycling.

Non-working device

Route it to appropriate recycling or processing.

Damaged or swollen battery

Do not dismantle it yourself. Seek appropriate specialized handling.
