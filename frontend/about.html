ABOUT E-CYCLE

Making E-Waste Management Simpler, Smarter and More Responsible

E-Cycle is a community-focused digital platform that makes electronic-waste management easier, more transparent and accessible. Our goal is simple: give every electronic device a responsible next step instead of letting it become unmanaged waste.

WHAT WE DO

Practical tools for responsible e-waste action

From the first submission to documented processing, E-Cycle helps users understand and follow the journey.

♻️

Responsible E-Waste Management

Encourage reuse, repair, donation, refurbishment and recycling based on device condition.

📍

Convenient Collection

Submit device details, request a pickup and select an available collection slot.

📊

Transparent Tracking

Use tracking IDs, QR verification and dashboards to follow recorded collection activity.

🤝

Community Participation

Help households, students and organisations take part in responsible e-waste initiatives.

OUR PURPOSE

Making responsible e-waste action easier for everyone

E-Cycle bridges the gap between people who want to dispose of electronics responsibly and the collection and processing operations that support proper waste management.

Our Mission

Make e-waste management accessible through digital submissions, collection coordination, tracking and processing records.

Our Vision

Support a sustainable circular economy by promoting reuse, repair, refurbishment and responsible recycling.

Our Core Values

Responsibility, transparency, accessibility, accountability and sustainability guide the platform.

Our Impact

Dashboards present recorded submissions, collection requests, completed pickups, electronic items, community drives, distributor activity, facility activity and documented handovers.

Environmental benefits are estimates unless supported by validated calculations or independent evidence.

Our Technology

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Python, Flask, SQLite, secure sessions, unique tracking IDs, QR verification, role-based dashboards, maps and Gemini-powered guidance where configured.

The platform is deployed with Gunicorn and Render.

Our Commitment

  • Protect user data with authentication and access restrictions.
  • Display accurate submission and collection statuses.
  • Encourage appropriately authorised processing partners.
  • Separate sample records from real operational records.
  • Never present internal records as statutory certificates.
LEGAL & ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

Responsible by design. Accountable in practice.

E-Cycle supports responsible e-waste awareness and record-keeping in alignment with the principles of India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 and applicable amendments. Requirements vary by collection, transportation, storage, refurbishment and recycling activity.

E-Cycle is a community e-waste management platform, not a government authority or a substitute for statutory registration and certification.
