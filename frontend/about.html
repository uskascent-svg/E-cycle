ABOUT E-CYCLE

Making E-Waste Management Simpler

E-Cycle is a community-focused platform designed to guide people toward responsible handling of unwanted electronic devices.

♻️

Responsible Action

Encourage reuse, repair, donation, refurbishment and recycling instead of careless disposal.

📍

Collection

Users can request collection for submitted e-waste and follow its status.

📊

Community Impact

Show platform-recorded submissions, collection activity and community participation.

OUR PURPOSE

Making responsible e-waste action easier

E-Cycle connects people, collection operations and responsible processing records in one transparent digital workflow.

Our Mission

Make electronic-waste disposal responsible, accessible and understandable for households, communities and organisations.

Our Vision

Support a cleaner, sustainable circular economy where devices are reused, repaired, refurbished or recycled through accountable channels.

THE JOURNEY

How E-Cycle works

A clear path from unwanted device to responsible next step.

01

Submit

Describe the device, condition, category and pickup details.

02

Schedule

Choose an available collection slot after sign-in.

03

Track

Use the tracking ID and status history to follow collection.

04

Process

Record handover and responsible processing through authorised partners.

Our Impact

Platform dashboards report recorded submissions, pickups, quantities, community activity and documented processing. Environmental figures are labelled as estimates unless independently validated.

View impact dashboard →

Our Technology

Flask, SQLite, responsive web pages, secure sessions, tracking tokens, QR verification, role-based dashboards, operational maps and AI-assisted guidance where configured.

Our Commitment

Protect user data, show honest statuses, separate sample records, verify partners and make the difference between an E-Cycle processing record and an official regulatory certificate clear.

LEGAL & ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

Responsible by design

E-Cycle is designed to support responsible collection and traceability. Applicable registration, transport, storage, processing and partner requirements depend on the real operating model.
