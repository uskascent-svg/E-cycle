ABOUT E-CYCLE

Making E-Waste Management Simpler, Smarter and More Responsible

E-Cycle is a community-driven platform that simplifies electronic waste management through responsible disposal, convenient collection and transparent tracking.

Our goal: Give every electronic device a responsible next step.

Our Technology

Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
Backend: Python, Flask
Database: SQLite
Security: Password hashing and secure sessions
Features: QR tracking, dashboards, maps and Gemini AI guidance where configured
Deployment: Gunicorn and Render

Our Commitment

  • Protect user information through access controls.
  • Maintain transparent collection records.
  • Encourage authorised processing partners.
  • Distinguish real activity from demo data.
  • Never misrepresent records as statutory certificates.
LEGAL & ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

Responsible by Design. Accountable in Practice.

E-Cycle supports responsible e-waste awareness in alignment with India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 and applicable amendments.

Requirements depend on the activities performed. E-Cycle is a community platform, not a government authority or substitute for statutory registration.
